What a leverage check is, in plain words

  • Leverage means knowing something the other side of the table assumes you do not.
  • It scores four areas of your sourcing, 1 to 10, from questions you tap.
  • It names the part of Lytica accountable for closing each gap it finds.

The four areas it scores

  • Price truth. Can you tell a fair price from a bad one before you agree it?
  • Supply concentration. How much of your line depends on one factory?
  • Design stage reach. Is the part locked before you get a say?
  • Negotiation preparation. What do you walk into the room holding?
